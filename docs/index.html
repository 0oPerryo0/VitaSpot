Questions, answered

The practical details about accounts, playback, controls, and what lives on your Vita.

Do I need Spotify Premium?+

Yes. VitaSpot requires a Spotify Premium account. Free accounts are not supported for playback.

Is VitaSpot an official Spotify app?+

No. VitaSpot is unofficial and is not affiliated with Spotify. It uses librespot and private protocol endpoints, which break Spotify's Terms of Use. It is intended for personal use only.

How do I sign in?+

Connect your Vita and phone or computer to the same WiFi network. Open Spotify's device picker and choose PS Vita. VitaSpot signs in and remembers the account.

What can I play and control?+

You can browse your library, search songs, artists, albums and playlists, manage the queue, seek, shuffle, repeat, and listen at up to 320 kbps with gapless playback.

Does VitaSpot support lyrics?+

Yes, when Spotify provides lyrics for the track. Synced lyrics highlight the current line, and tapping a line jumps to that moment.

Can I use buttons instead of touch?+

Yes. The interface supports the directional pad, analog stick, action buttons, shoulder buttons, Start, and Select. The complete control map is in the project README.

Where does VitaSpot store my data?+

On the Vita, credentials, settings, logs, and media caches live in ux0:data/VitaSpot. This website has no accounts, analytics, cookies, or tracking.

Can I build it from source?+

Yes. The frontend uses C11 and SDL2, while the backend uses Rust and librespot. The README includes VitaSDK, desktop, mock backend, and real backend build instructions.
